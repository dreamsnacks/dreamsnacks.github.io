# Baku artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for this game. The reference images informed the quiet paper/ink scenery and indigo, plum, rose and teal palette; the character is an original illustration.

- Original transparent master: `baku-dream-eater-v1.png`
- Optimized transparent sprite: `baku-dream-eater-v1.webp` (512 × 504)
- The WebP is embedded in `index.html`, so the game also works offline as one file.
- The sleeping cat, scenery, menus, patterned orbs and dream-breath animation are drawn in Canvas.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated production game character sprite, true transparent background. Create an ORIGINAL Japanese Baku dream-eater for an ink-and-color marble-shooter. Full animal body in a compact seated/crouching three-quarter pose, slight view from above, head to the left and forward, stable horizontal orientation. It must read as an animal chimera, NOT a human face: long elegant elephant/tapir snout with a gently upward-curled trunk, bear-like powerful shoulders and haunches, tiger paws with restrained bold stripes and ivory claws, pointed tufted ears, a small knowing almond eye. Mature and mischievous rather than baby-cute; no huge eyes, no plush-toy proportions. Strong silhouette, good readability at 80 pixels, feet together under the body, the entire subject within a square with generous transparent margin. Color: rich clearly separated near-black indigo head and legs, saturated violet/plum body saddle, subtle teal-blue edge washes, warm ivory muzzle and tiger-stripe accents; a little dusty rose inside the ear. The colors should be vividly pigmented and distinguishable, not gray/muddy and not neon gradients. Traditional Japanese ink wash meets textured gouache and restrained woodblock-print hatch work: confident dry-brush edges, grain in the pigment, broad flat shapes, beautiful negative space and a few ivory star-like speckles on the dark coat. Keep details concentrated at face and paws. No backdrop, no ground plane, no drop shadow, no mist/clouds, no other animals, no ball or orb, no props, no text, no seals, no signature, no watermark, no border. This sprite will have separate bright dream orbs drawn around it in the game.
